# Rwanda — the 2022 census

The Fifth Population and Housing Census (RPHC5), August 2022, by NISR, the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda.

- `counts.csv`: the country, its 4 provinces and the City of Kigali, and their 30 districts, with men, women and
  total, from Table 1 of the *RPHC5 2022 Main Indicators* workbook. Read from the workbook's cells, not typed.
- `units.csv`: each district's unit in geoBoundaries gbOpen RWA ADM2, all 30 by the same name.
- `provenance.json`: the workbook, its digest, the licence (CC BY 4.0, NISR's terms of use) and the checks: men plus
  women equals the total on all 36 rows, each province's districts sum to it, and the provinces sum to 13,246,394.

Cite as: National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR), Fifth Rwanda Population and Housing Census 2022, Main
Indicators, Table 1. CC BY 4.0.
